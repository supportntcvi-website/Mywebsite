About Us

Building a strategic bridge between Japanese expertise and the Indian market since 1982.

Our Heritage

Founded in 2002 by Mr. Bhisham Dev Nayyar, who brings over 40 years of extensive experience in Indo-Japan business relations, C V International Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2017 to foster and strengthen strategic trade partnerships between India and Japan. With deep industry knowledge and decades of collaboration with Japanese trading enterprises, the company was created to facilitate seamless, long-standing business relationships across Asia.

Our operations are guided by integrity, transparency, and a commitment to quality. C V International Pvt Ltd. stands as a trusted business consultant and strategic partner, facilitating cross-border coordination, market entry, and supply chain management between India and Japan
